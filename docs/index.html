Prime Number Studies

Independent re-derivations, measurements and negative results around the division table

Mohamed Osman · ORCID 0009-0004-5912-999X · independent researcher

Every headline result here already exists in the literature, and each paper says so and names the source. What the collection offers is a route followed honestly end to end, negative results stated plainly rather than buried, and a script in code/ that regenerates every number printed. No claim is made about the Riemann Hypothesis.
